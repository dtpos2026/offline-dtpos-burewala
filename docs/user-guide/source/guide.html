1.1About DT POS Enterprise

A complete point-of-sale and back-office system for restaurants, cafés, fast-food outlets and takeaways — installed on a Windows computer and working fully offline.

What it does

DT POS Enterprise runs the whole service day in one program: taking orders at the counter or at the table, sending tickets to the kitchen, printing bills, taking cash, card and online payments, keeping credit (udhaar) accounts, tracking stock and food cost, paying staff and suppliers, and closing the day with a clean report.

Who uses it

  • Cashiers take orders and payments at the POS screen, retrieve open bills and reprint receipts.
  • Managers look after tables, deliveries, the menu, stock, customers, staff and reports.
  • The owner / administrator sets the restaurant up, manages users and permissions, and closes the day.
  • The kitchen works from printed tickets (KOT) or from the Kitchen Display on a screen or TV.

How it works

  • Offline first. All data is kept on the computer. Billing, printing and reports never wait for the internet.
  • Licensed per computer. A licence key from Digital Target activates the software on each PC.
  • One screen per job. Each module opens from the menu on the left; managers decide which modules each user can see.
  • Thermal printing. 80 mm and 58 mm receipt printers, separate kitchen and token printers, and many receipt designs.

Highlights

  • Fast billing with keyboard shortcuts, size and weight items, deals, discounts, promo codes and service charge.
  • Dine-in floor plan with table transfer, merge and split; delivery board and self-pickup.
  • Kitchen Display and Customer Display for a second screen or TV.
  • Credit customers with full ledger, partial payments and recovery.
  • Inventory with recipes, receiving (GRN), wastage and food-cost reports.
  • Accounts, party ledgers, daily wages, HR, attendance and payslips.
  • Dashboard and more than fifteen reports, with PDF, Excel and 80 mm print.
  • Twenty-one built-in looks, including the DT Retail themes, plus your own custom theme.

First start

2.1Starting the software

DT POS Enterprise is installed on a Windows PC by Digital Target or your dealer. Open it from the desktop shortcut.

Each time the software starts, the splash screen shows the product name, your restaurant's name and the version while the data is loaded from the computer.

Installing

  1. Run the DT POS Enterprise setup file supplied by Digital Target.
  2. Connect and switch on the receipt printer (and the kitchen printer, if you have one) before the first start, so Windows lists them.
  3. Start the software. On a new computer the activation screen appears first (next page).
Tip. Keep the computer's date and time correct. The licence refuses to open if the clock is turned back by more than three hours.
Data stays on this PC. Orders, menu, customers and settings are saved on the computer itself, so the POS keeps working without internet. Take regular backups (chapter 17).

2.2Activating the licence

The software runs only on a computer activated with a valid DT POS licence key.

  1. Type the Business Name, the Owner Name and the Mobile Number.
  2. Enter the License Key exactly as received — it starts with DTPOS-.
  3. Press Activate License. The sign-in screen opens.

The code at the bottom right of the card identifies this computer. Press it to copy it, then send it to Digital Target if support asks for it.

After activation

  • The licence is checked on the computer itself each time the software opens, so it works offline.
  • When the PC is online, the status is refreshed in the background (for example a renewal or a suspension by Digital Target).
  • A licence limited to a number of devices refuses extra computers.
Status. Licence keys, renewals and device limits are issued by Digital Target from its own administration console. That console is not part of the restaurant software and is not covered in this guide.

2.4Quick sign-in with a PIN

A busy counter can switch cashiers in two seconds: tap your name and type a 4-digit PIN. The password always keeps working.

  1. Press Sign in with PIN under the Sign In button.
  2. Tap your name card. Then type your PIN on the keypad or the keyboard — the fourth digit signs you in.
  3. A user without a PIN is taken to the password form with the username filled in.

The PIN is set per user in Users → Edit user → Quick login PIN (chapter 15.5). No one gets a PIN automatically.

Safety. After five wrong PINs, that user's PIN sign-in is locked for one minute. The password still works.

4.3The bill, step by step

The bill panel holds every action for the current order. Each numbered part is explained here.

  1. 1Order type — Dining / Takeaway / Delivery. Locked while an existing bill is being edited.
  2. 2Customer — name and phone. Typing suggests saved customers; the bill then appears in their history and loyalty.
  3. 3Lines — item, quantity, line total. Tap a line to select it for the keypad.
  4. 4Totals — preset discount buttons (−5% … −200), Discount in Rs or %, Promo code, Service charge (+ Add), Total, then Payment received and Change. The Keypad below enters quantities, prices or cash.
  5. 5Hold (F2) — parks the order unpaid, for example for a customer who will come back. CLR empties the bill.
  6. 6PAY (F4 or Enter) — opens the payment screen.
  7. 7Kitchen — saves the order as running and shows the kitchen slip. Customer Receipt — shows the bill to print or hand over.
  8. 8Running (F3) — saves the order as open (sent to the kitchen) without payment. When editing an existing bill this reads Update.
  9. 9More actions — Credit (udhaar), Void, Cancel Edit, and the cart width.

A typical takeaway
